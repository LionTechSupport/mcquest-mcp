| 3 | Alias/parameter canonicalization for duplicate detection | excluded from A9 (needs a maintained alias table and drift protection) |

| 8 | Line-ending condition in `tests/test_registration.py` | separate repository-integrity issue; outside P0 scope and not repaired |
