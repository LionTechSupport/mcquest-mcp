Positive, negative (with byte-identity), bounds, and integrity classes are
each covered by at least one test; security-critical cases (traversal,

## 8. Known skip

`test_symlink_escape_rejected` skips when Windows denies unprivileged
`os.symlink`. The confinement guarantee does not depend on the skip: when the
link can be created, the test proves rejection; when it cannot, no symlink
attack surface exists in that environment.
